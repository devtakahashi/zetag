Terms of Service

What ZeTag provides

ZeTag lets you create and share payment requests and connect a Phantom wallet. A request link does not confirm that funds were sent. USDC transfers and Zelle Tag linking are currently unavailable.

Using tags and request links

Use a name you’re entitled to use. Do not impersonate another person or business or create misleading payment requests. Tags saved in your browser are not globally registered or verified.

Wallet safety

Review requests and wallet prompts carefully. Keep your recovery phrase and private keys in your own control. ZeTag’s wallet connection asks for your public address.
