Privacy Policy

Information stored on your device

When you create a request, ZeTag saves your chosen tag and display name in this browser. You can remove them by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

Payment request links

A request link contains the recipient tag and any amount, display name, or note you include. Anyone with the link can read those details. Keep personal and confidential information out of the note.

Wallet connection and site delivery

If you connect Phantom, ZeTag reads your public wallet address to display it while the page is open. Phantom handles wallet permissions. GitHub Pages hosts the site, and Google Fonts supplies its typefaces. Those services may receive connection data when your browser requests their resources.
