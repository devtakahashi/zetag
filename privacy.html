Privacy Policy

Information stored on your device

When you create a request, ZeTag saves your chosen tag and display name in this browser. You can remove them by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

Payment request links

A request link contains the recipient tag and any amount, display name, or note you include. Anyone with the link can read those details. Keep personal and confidential information out of the note. If you upload a logo, ZeTag resizes it in your browser and includes the image in the share link. Older links can contain an external logo URL, which may connect your browser to that image host.

Wallet connection and site delivery

If you connect a wallet, ZeTag reads its public address and queries a Solana RPC provider for the public USDC balance and recent transactions. Your wallet handles connection permissions. GitHub Pages hosts the site, and Google Fonts supplies its typefaces. Those services may receive connection data when your browser requests their resources.
